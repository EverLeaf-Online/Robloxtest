Privacy Policy

Effective September 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how EverLeaf Online processes information when you use the Scrap-to-Bot Factory Roblox experience and its related Discord application and services ("Services").

1. Information we may process

Depending on the feature you use, the Services may process information supplied by Roblox or Discord, including user IDs, usernames or display names, server/guild IDs, channel IDs, role information, command or interaction data, game identifiers, and timestamps. We may also process limited technical and operational logs needed for reliability, abuse prevention, debugging, and security.

2. How information is used

We use information only as reasonably necessary to operate and improve the Services, respond to commands and interactions, link supported game and Discord features, prevent abuse, investigate errors, enforce rules, and protect the Services and their users.

3. Information we do not request

The Discord application does not request your Discord password. We do not ask users to provide payment-card details through the Discord application. Purchases made through Roblox are handled by Roblox under Roblox's own privacy and payment practices.

4. Storage and retention

Operational data may be stored for as long as it is reasonably needed to provide the relevant feature, maintain security, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, or investigate abuse. Temporary logs may be deleted or rotated sooner. We aim to avoid retaining personal information that is no longer needed.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers or hosting infrastructure used to operate the Services, and may be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the Services, or legal rights.

6. Platform services

Your use of Roblox and Discord is also governed by their respective privacy policies. Those platforms may independently collect and process information about your account and activity.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to the Services. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Your requests

You may request information about data associated with you, or request deletion where applicable, by contacting us through the issue tracker linked below. We may need enough information to verify the request and locate relevant records.

9. Children's privacy

The Services are intended to be used only in accordance with Roblox and Discord age requirements and platform protections. We do not knowingly use the Discord application to solicit sensitive personal information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Services, our practices, or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when material revisions are published.

11. Contact

Privacy questions or requests can be submitted through the Scrap-to-Bot Factory repository issue tracker. Do not post sensitive personal information in a public issue; request a private contact method if needed.

Scrap-to-Bot Factory is an independent project. Roblox and Discord are trademarks of their respective owners, and EverLeaf Online is not affiliated with or endorsed by either company.
